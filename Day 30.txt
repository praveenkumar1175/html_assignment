2. Function Accepting a Callback
function greet(name, callback){
console.log("Hello " + name);
callback();
}

function afterGreeting(){
console.log("Welcome to Online Shopping");
}

greet("Praveen", afterGreeting);
Output:
Hello Praveen
Welcome to Online Shopping
